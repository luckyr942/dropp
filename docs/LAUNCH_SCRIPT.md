## PHASE 3 — WHAT MAKES AURA DIFFERENT (1:45–2:15)

[Architecture slide: Aura → Orchestrator → Ride · Food · Merchant → live updates to all apps]

Here's what's behind it.

**One:** Aura turns Hindi, Hinglish or English into structured service requests.

**Two:** our orchestration layer coordinates the services, but each one keeps its own rules and lifecycle. If one fails, the other doesn't go down with it.

**Three:** prices, stock and booking state are checked on the server, and every change streams live to every app.

## PHASE 4 — THE BIGGER VISION (2:15–2:45)

[Slide: Dropp in the centre, connected through MCP to rides, food, flights, reminders]

And Dropp is already open. We expose it through the Model Context Protocol, so any AI assistant can book a ride or run a store on Dropp today.

Next, Aura connects outward, to compatible services like food platforms, flights and reminders, through the same protocol.

Imagine booking a ride, checking your flight and ordering coffee, all in one conversation.

The goal isn't to replace every service.

**It's to make the services people already use work together.**
